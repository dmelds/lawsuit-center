Site Privacy Information

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how Lawsuit Center may collect, use, and handle information submitted through the site.

Published March 22, 2026 · Updated October 12, 2026.

Last updated: October 2026 · Lawsuit Center

Information you provide

When you submit a form through Lawsuit Center, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your situation or inquiry.

Information collected automatically

Like many websites, Lawsuit Center may collect limited technical and usage information automatically. This may include browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, and general site activity data.

How information is used

Submitted information may be used to review inquiries, better understand potential legal situations, respond to messages, improve site content, and determine whether further follow-up may be appropriate.

Information sharing

Information you submit through a case review form is referred by David Meldofsky, a California attorney, to participating law firms that have active intake in the relevant lawsuit category and your state. Contact-form messages stay with Lawsuit Center unless you ask us to pass them on.

Where a participating firm takes a case from a case review, it may pay a referral fee to David Meldofsky, the attorney who built Lawsuit Center. The terms are in our Advertising Disclosure. The fee does not change what information is shared or affect a visitor's privacy rights.

Lawsuit Center may also share information with service providers that help operate the site — such as form submission hosts, analytics providers, and customer-relationship platforms — subject to the providers' own terms.

Submitting information does not guarantee representation, case acceptance, or follow-up.

Third-party services

Lawsuit Center may use third-party services to operate the site, manage form submissions, host content, or analyze website activity. These services may process information as part of providing those functions.

Analytics and tracking

Lawsuit Center may use analytics tools to better understand site traffic and visitor activity. These tools may use cookies, scripts, or similar technologies to help measure site usage and improve the website.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to support site functionality, understand traffic patterns, and improve the user experience. By continuing to use the site, you acknowledge that such technologies may be used.

Data storage and security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information submitted through the site. However, no method of internet transmission, website storage, or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Voluntary submission

Submitting information through Lawsuit Center is voluntary. Please do not send highly sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary personal information through the site unless you are comfortable doing so.

No attorney-client relationship

Submitting information through this site does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Third-party links

This site may contain links to third-party websites. Lawsuit Center is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of outside websites.

Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes or as practices are refined. Continued use of the site after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please visit the contact page.
